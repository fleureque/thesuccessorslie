{Velmonta} Blurb: Taken off the streets of Scab City as an ‘expendable pawn’, you must adapt. You were smart for the streets, but in this hostile environment, no longer the smartest in the room. Things are hardly ever as simple as they appear. Unfortunately for you, you finds yourself in the main line of Velmonta’s household, where external forces seek to claim her spot your successor candidate. And now, in this lion’s den, you must rely on luck, foresight, and performance just to survive. All this with something crucial to protect. Most of all, you have a personal debt to fulfil with Emperor Leander IV, who abandoned you and everyone else in the Lower District. Despite hating the hypocrisy of the nobles, you're forced to emulate it. What does it mean to survive? In a world where even the church is nothing more than a construct, does true morality even exist?
